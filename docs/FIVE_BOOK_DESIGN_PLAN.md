# Five guidebook prototypes

The baseline failed its visual review: overprinted dark search text, clipped labels, generic modifier rules before the useful explanation, a footer outside the cover, and sentences cut across columns. A successful replacement must help a player **do something**, not merely contain all the text.

## Candidates

| Book item | Information architecture | Intended advantage | Principal risk to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Field Journal | Two paper pages, contents opposite an obvious first-dig entry | Familiar book form, balanced browsing | Narrower reading columns |
| Workshop Manual | Persistent category sidebar and task list | Fewer detours between explanation and categories | Sidebar takes reading width |
| Trail Guide | Six task cards ordered from finding a relic to ascension; category ribbon | A clear route for a new player without locking content | Veterans may not need the suggested route |
| Pocket Reference | Persistent result list beside the selected article | Fast repeated lookup and comparison | Dense at small window sizes |
| Relic Atlas | Illustrated category cards and a section rail | Low reading burden when selecting a subject | More category-to-entry navigation |

Every candidate is a separately registered item with a distinct generated inventory icon. All read exactly the same 27 chapters and 120 live modifier definitions. The comparison is about presentation, not different knowledge or gameplay. Three finalists will appear together at the start of the mod creative tab; other prototypes remain available through `/give` for review.

## Five simulated player perspectives

These are a structured heuristic evaluation performed by the developer assistant, **not five human participants**. Native automated input verifies the interactions; screenshot review judges presentation. Success of the scripts alone does not constitute a usability pass.

| Persona | Minecraft / mods experience | Task and critical questions |
|---|---|---|
| Rowan, newcomer | A few hours / none | Can I find the first practical action? Does it say where artifacts come from and what to do next? Does the icon clearly look like a usable book? |
| Alex, vanilla veteran | Years / first content mod | Can I find Sniffer requirements, Cruncher food, and hatching help using ordinary words? Are unfamiliar ideas explained rather than assumed? |
| Morgan, casual mod player | Comfortable / several small mods | Can I switch from excavation instructions to tools and back without losing my place? Are recovery and quality easy to find? |
| Sam, modpack builder | Expert / many complex mods | Can I look up effect, eligibility, caps, tier weights, growth and ascension resources without reading generic prose repeatedly? |
| Casey, returning player | Experienced / returning after a break, small window | Can I search, recover from no results, use keyboard focus, reopen where I left off, and read at 854×480 without clipped controls? |

## Acceptance and selection

- A named first action on Home; seven visible subject categories; global search on every screen.
- Modifier effect first. Named sections put tiers, growth and costs one action away.
- Search is preserved by Back; changing sections stays in the article; related links have full labels.
- Actual native mouse and keyboard controls work. Escape, Close and bookmarks work for every item.
- Every body line and control stays within the page. No overlapping controls. Long sections paginate.
- A legible, recognizable inventory-size book icon, distinguished by symbol as well as cover color.
- No blocker for any persona. Score readability, navigation, explanation, icon and room to grow; select the three best balanced candidates after actual screenshot review.

See the completed review for evidence, revisions, scoring and limitations. The final choice remains the user's.
