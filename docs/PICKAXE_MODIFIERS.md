# Reconstructed pickaxe modifier reference

Exactly **100 ordinary modifiers** (including 12 shared-tool modifiers) and **20 influence-only modifiers** (10 Nether and 10 End). Generated from [`tools/pickaxe-modifiers.json`](../tools/pickaxe-modifiers.json). Edit that file and run `python3 tools/generate_pickaxe_catalog.py`; `--check` rejects stale generated files.

## Rolling and component roles

Handles and connectors roll only the 12 **Universal** entries. They work on all seven reconstructed tool types. The three pickaxe head pieces roll the other 88 ordinary modifiers and, after the Influencer, their matching 10 exclusive modifiers. Saved old rolls are never rerolled; old mining rolls on shared pieces still work on pickaxes but are ignored on other reconstructed tools.

Family weight selects a modifier first; the number of its tiers never changes its frequency. Tier weight then selects a value band, followed by a uniform value inside that band. T1 is strongest. The six weights in each band are Common/Uncommon/Rare/Epic/Legendary/Unique. A zero means unavailable. Listed weights are relative, not percentages: actual odds depend on component role, rarity, exclusivity groups, existing rolls and influence. Matching influence multiplies family weight by six; foreign tags are excluded. Uninfluenced pieces may roll ordinary tagged modifiers but never exclusive ones. Influence is assigned once, only by the Influencer; no repeat rolls, switches, removals or annulment reset.

Rarity controls independent modifier-count attempts: C `[1]`, U `[1,.20]`, R `[1,.45,.10]`, E `[1,.65,.25]`, L `[1,.80,.50,.15]`, Unique `[1,.90,.70,.35]`. This expansion does not add new artifact drop chances. Epic+ ancient runes still offer three fixed legal choices, and lock/corruption rules remain enforced.

## Quality, growth and milestones

Numeric contribution = `base roll × quality/100 × (1 + ascensions × grade ascension bonus + awarded levels × grade level bonus)`. Contributions sum before the listed hard cap. Quality ranges 0–120%; 0% never grants an effect. A level awards 1–3 randomly selected numeric contributions a small growth increment. Base values, tiers and grades remain separately saved, and original recovered scores remain immutable.

| Grade | Per ascension | Per selected level | Milestone thresholds |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| F | +3% of base | +0.025% of base | ascensions 4,7,10 |
| D | +5% | +0.05% | 4,7,10 |
| C | +7.5% | +0.075% | 3,6,9 |
| B | +10% | +0.1% | 3,6,9 |
| A | +15% | +0.15% | 2,5,8 |
| S | +20% | +0.2% | 2,5,8 |

Milestone perks start at stage 0, then advance through stages 1/2/3 at the thresholds above. They do not receive meaningless numeric level increments. A qualifying perk's activation chance is its component quality (capped at 100%; multiple copies sum), and its stage is the strongest qualifying copy. A zero-quality copy cannot enable a stage. Each break/tick rolls an effect at most once; cooldowns and resonance costs still apply. Stage values below are stage 0/1/2/3. No-upgrade binary perks remain binary at every stage.

Resonance is per-tool charge (0–100), saved separately from immutable parts. Primary breaks and movement can generate it; secondary breaks cannot farm charges, duplicate resources, award tool XP or recurse. Charge consumers run in documented source order, so a charge budget forces choices. Area perks share a hard limit of twelve native extra breaks per primary break and only run when crouching, keeping single-block mining available. Secondary breaks still use normal Fortune, Silk and furnace handling. All extra targets reject block entities, unbreakable/incorrect-tool blocks, unloaded cells, out-of-border cells and canceled native break events. Utility placement uses native placement cancellation and spends inventory materials only after success.

`Numeric` below uses the formula above; `Milestone` uses the thresholds and stage effects above. Resource cost is two of the listed item per rolled occurrence per upcoming ascension, except Long Reach (one pearl). Identical resource costs combine. Existing 0/50/100 Creative templates roll from the correct new role pools when obtained; old sealed templates retain their exact rolls.

## Interaction examples and practical limits

- Gem Battery + Quarry Discharge turns gem mining into occasional extra excavation. Experience Battery feeds charge by spending mining XP instead of creating more XP.
- Phasebound + Ender Dispatch collects into your inventory first and sends overflow to your Ender Chest; Dimensional Battery rewards successful direct collection. Full containers leave exact remainders on the ground.
- Smelter/Pyroclastic + Cinder Chain turns furnace processing into resonance. Soul Furnace trades player XP for reliable furnace processing.
- Broadhead + Lightbearer + Careful Mason builds a lit tunnel while consuming fewer of your own torches. Crouching enables area effects; standing mining remains precise.
- Planted Stance + Precision Silk favors deliberate excavation; Mining Rhythm + Alternating Current favors moving through mixed strata.
- Spoils Ward protects mined drops temporarily and restores their previous invulnerability, including if you stop holding the tool.

## Testing a build

The existing Creative entries supply random parts at 0%, 50% and 100% quality using the correct component pools. Recover or grab the three pickaxe head pieces plus a connector and handle, inspect their rolls, and assemble them at the Reconstruction Table. Crouch when mining to enable area perks. Resonance appears on the tool tooltip when charged. Hold Shift and scroll over the tooltip to browse every eight-modifier page; numeric bonuses show their effective value, while milestone perks show their current stage. The component stations continue to operate on pieces, never on assembled tools. An influenced handle or connector still uses only its neutral universal pool; current influence-only effects belong to pickaxe head pieces. Existing sealed parts retain their old rolls.

Charge processing order is XP conversion, loot consumers (Spoils Discharge, Temporal Echo, Endstone Alchemy), generators (battery, gems, furnace, magma, alternating chain), Haste/repair/allied sharing, collection rewards, combat and mobility, placement, and finally area mining. An earlier spender may leave too little charge for a later effect. Conditional speed bonuses sum to at most +800%, separately from the +600% base-speed cap and native penalty recovery.

There is no recursive area mining, player targeting, arbitrary thrown-item vacuuming, container-loot copying, XP duplication, free inventory conversion or permanent drop invulnerability. Destructive area perks are deliberately bounded; transformations and bonus drops are disabled for Silk Touch and secondary breaks. Fortune remains native capped III. Tooltips show only effective modifiers; shared equipment uses a general-tool header rather than fake pickaxe progression. Numeric caps constrain combined builds, so multiple large rolls can saturate a stat. This is an initial balance pass intended for later tuning.
