# Reconstructed pickaxe modifier reference

The catalog contains **100 ordinary modifiers**, including 12 universal handle/connector rolls, plus **10 Nether-only and 10 End-only modifiers**. Edit `tools/pickaxe-modifiers.json` and `tools/modifier-gameplay.json`, then run `python3 tools/generate_pickaxe_catalog.py`. CI checks generated Java, descriptions and this reference.

## A tool worth reconstructing

A reconstructed pickaxe receives an innate mining multiplier of **1 + 2 × average quality of its three head pieces**: 1× at 0%, 2× at 50%, 3× at 100%, and 3.4× at 120%. This multiplies the native mining speed, including Efficiency and Haste; native water/airborne penalties still apply. It does not replace or remove enchantments. At 100% quality, a Diamond-speed restored pickaxe with Efficiency V has a 102 base speed versus 35 for a Netherite Efficiency V pickaxe, before its rolled modifiers. A strong unenchanted speed build can also beat that vanilla benchmark. Actual break times still depend on hardness, correct tool, penalties and the one-tick minimum.

Fortune rolls add to the native enchantment up to **Fortune X**, preserving an existing stronger enchantment. This pass does not change artifact acquisition/drop chances. Existing recovered parts keep their original seeds, raw rolls, tiers, grades, quality, locks, influence and corruption; the stronger effect conversion applies to old parts too. New high-rarity rolls favor strong tiers and more modifier attempts without guaranteeing a particular result.

Rarity modifier-count attempts: Common `[1]`, Uncommon `[1,.35]`, Rare `[1,.8,.35]`, Epic `[1,.95,.7]`, Legendary `[1,.99,.9,.6]`, Unique `[1,.999,.97,.85]`. A family is selected by its weight first, then a tier by that rarity's tier weights, then a uniform value inside its band. T1 is strongest. The six weights in each band below are Common/Uncommon/Rare/Epic/Legendary/Unique. They are relative weights, not fixed probabilities. Matching influence receives 6× family weight; foreign influence is excluded. Influence is permanent and can only be applied once at the Influencer. No influence label is added to ordinary component tooltips.

Handles/connectors use only the twelve **Universal** modifiers, which work on all seven reconstructed tool types. Head pieces use the mining pool. Saved old mining rolls on handles still work on pickaxes, but do not affect other tools.

## Quality and growth

Numeric contribution = `raw roll × effect conversion × quality/100 × (1 + ascensions × grade bonus + selected-level awards × level bonus)`, capped after summing contributions. The displayed bands below include the effect conversion; saved raw rolls remain stable. Quality is 0–120%; a zero-quality piece grants no modifier effects or milestone stages. Each ordinary level selects 1–3 positive numeric contributions for a small increment. Perks advance by ascension milestones instead.

| Grade | Per ascension, relative to base | Per selected level | Perk ascension thresholds |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| F | +6% | +0.05% | 4 / 7 / 10 |
| D | +10% | +0.10% | 4 / 7 / 10 |
| C | +15% | +0.15% | 3 / 6 / 9 |
| B | +22% | +0.25% | 3 / 6 / 9 |
| A | +32% | +0.35% | 2 / 5 / 8 |
| S | +45% | +0.50% | 2 / 5 / 8 |

A perk activates with its combined quality-adjusted chance, capped at 100%, and uses the strongest positive-quality copy's stage. Stage values below mean stage 0 / 1 / 2 / 3. Cooldowns are per tool. Every reconstructed pickaxe has a **200 resonance** capacity; primary mining and holding the tool generate quality-scaled core charge, so consumers function without requiring another lucky generator roll. Dedicated generators enable more frequent combined use.

Crouch to enable area mining. All area effects share at most **64 extra native breaks** per primary break within a loaded 16-block range. No recursion, extra tool XP, primary bonus drops or charge generation from secondary breaks. Secondary loot still uses native Fortune, Silk, furnace recipes and the five material conversions. Block entities, claims, canceled events, unbreakable blocks, wrong tools, unloaded chunks and the world border are respected. Utility placement consumes owned materials only after a successful cancellable placement. Silk disables duplication, bonus drops and furnace conversions.

Use the **Appraisal Table** for live values, sources, stage effects, growth, caps and ascension materials. Tooltips show a compact summary. Appraisal is read-only and does not reveal sealed ancient rune choices or roll unopened artifacts.
